Your music, lossless. With a DJ when you want one.

Fresh is a music player for the files you own and the music servers you run, on iPhone and iPad. No account, no streaming catalogue, no tracking.

View on the App Store

Requires iOS 27 or later.

What it does

Lossless and hi-res

FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, DSD (DSF and DFF), Monkey's Audio, WavPack, Opus, Ogg Vorbis, MP3 and AAC. Gapless playback, ReplayGain and a 16-band equalizer with a parametric editor.

An honest audio path

Pure playback bypasses every effect. The Audio path screen lists anything that still changes the signal and says “Bit-perfect conditions met” only when nothing does. iOS system sounds can still mix in, and your DAC's volume handling can't be verified.

DJ mode

Fresh mixes from one song into the next from your own library. Tempo, beats and key are analysed on your device; no audio leaves it. A separate purchase.

Your servers and folders

Play from Files, iCloud Drive or a USB drive, or connect your own Jellyfin, Emby, Navidrome / Subsonic or Plex server, WebDAV and SMB folders, or DLNA servers. Download albums for offline play.

Everywhere you listen

CarPlay, an Apple Watch app, widgets, a Live Activity for DJ mixes, Siri and Shortcuts. In 36 languages.

Private by design

Your library, play history and DJ analysis stay on the device. Online lyrics (LRCLIB) and ListenBrainz scrobbling are off until you turn them on. Privacy policy
