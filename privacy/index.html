Fresh Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Overview

Fresh is a local music player. It does not require an account. Your music files are selected by you and are processed on your device. Fresh has no advertising, cross-app tracking, or automatically transmitted DJ telemetry. Optional DJ quality reporting is off by default.

Data stored on your device

Fresh reads the music folders and files you choose. It stores a security-scoped folder reference and a local library index containing file paths and music metadata such as title, artist, album, genre, disc number, and track number. It also stores playback and appearance preferences, crate organization, saved playlists (names, file references and track metadata), and local caches used for artwork, lyrics, waveform display, and DJ analysis. Fresh reads purchase-access state through StoreKit. The library index, preferences and caches stay in Fresh's app container. Your original music files remain in the location you selected.

Local library editing and radio

When you start Wi-Fi transfer, Fresh shares library metadata and selected covers with a computer presenting the session code on your local network. This HTTP connection is not encrypted. The server stops when you leave the transfer screen or Fresh leaves the foreground. Uploaded files remain in Documents/Fresh Imports. Local metadata edits, selected artwork and license attribution are stored on device. Optional file tag writing works on a temporary copy and replaces the original only after verification.

Personal radio stores a bounded local listening history, track preferences and transition decisions. These records are used on device for recommendations, are separate from optional quality-report counters and are not automatically transmitted. Fresh also keeps a local listening record (song, artist, genre, day and listening time, at most 400 days) for smart playlists and the yearly summary; it never leaves the device and is deleted with the app. A song you skip within 20 seconds of a DJ transition counts on device as a negative vote for that transition.

Optional music servers (Navidrome, Subsonic)

If you add a Navidrome or Subsonic server, Fresh stores the server address and user name on the device and the password in the device Keychain. Fresh contacts only that server, sending your user name, a salted password token and the album, song or song lyrics requested. Downloaded albums join your library in Fresh's Imports folder. Songs you play from the server without downloading are kept in a temporary cache that is replaced when you play the next album. Plain http is allowed only for addresses on your local network. Disconnect in Settings to delete the stored address, user name and password.

If you add a Jellyfin or Emby media server, Fresh sends your user name and password to that server once to sign in and keeps only the sign-in token it returns, in the device Keychain; the password is not stored. Fresh then asks that server for your music libraries, albums, artists, playlists and the songs you play or download. Disconnect in Settings to sign out and delete the token.

If you sign in with Plex to use a Plex Media Server, you sign in on plex.tv in your browser; Fresh never sees your Plex password. plex.tv (Plex, Inc.) sees that sign-in and a random client id Fresh makes once on the device, under Plex's own privacy policy, and tells Fresh which servers your account has. Fresh keeps only the sign-in token, in the device Keychain, and asks only your own server for its music, covers and the songs you play or download. Disconnect in Settings to delete the token.

If you use DLNA / UPnP media servers, Fresh asks the servers on your home network for their folders and the songs you play. Searching the network needs the local network permission; servers you add by address are remembered on the device. Nothing from these servers is sent anywhere else.

Cloud folders and history files

A music folder in a cloud drive (for example iCloud Drive) is read through the Files app; when a song is only online, the cloud drive's own app downloads it when you play it. If you import a listening history file (for example one you exported from Last.fm), Fresh reads it on your device to add play counts to your statistics; Fresh does not contact Last.fm.

Covers come only from your music files, images in your music folders, or an image you upload on the Wi-Fi transfer page. Fresh does not search for or download covers online.

Mixtapes

When you share a playlist as a mixtape, Fresh creates a small file with the playlist name and, for each song, its title, artist, album, length and an identifier derived from the file's location in your library. It contains no audio and no links. You choose the recipient in the iOS share sheet. Opening a mixtape compares it with your library on your device; nothing is sent or downloaded.

ListenBrainz (optional)

ListenBrainz scrobbling is off by default. If you connect it in Settings with your own ListenBrainz user token, Fresh sends the artist, title, album, length and play time of each song you play for at least half its length or 4 minutes, and the song playing now, directly from your device to api.listenbrainz.org, run by the MetaBrainz Foundation. Fresh has no server and receives none of it. Your listens appear publicly on your ListenBrainz profile, and MetaBrainz releases them under the CC0 licence. The token is stored in the device Keychain, and listens that could not be sent yet wait in a queue on the device. Turn sending off or log out in Settings; logging out deletes the token and the waiting listens. Delete listens already sent on listenbrainz.org. The MetaBrainz privacy policy applies to data on ListenBrainz.

DJ set history and described playlists

The tracklist of each DJ mix (song titles, artists, albums, times, tempo, key and transition type) is stored on the device only; no audio is recorded. It leaves the device only when you share a mix as text or as a mixtape. When you describe a playlist in words, the words are turned into playlist rules on your device (on devices with Apple Intelligence, by Apple's on-device model); nothing is sent.

Playlist import, moods, tag editing, covers and Apple Watch

When you import a playlist, Fresh reads the file you pick or, after you allow Media & Apple Music access, the song names of the Apple Music playlist you choose. Both are matched with your library on your device; nothing is sent. Mood tags you set are stored on the device only. When you edit tags, Fresh writes them to your own files only after you confirm a preview, keeps a copy of each file's previous tags on the device so you can undo, and checks that the audio is unchanged. A cover is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save to Photos, after you allow adding photos. The Apple Watch app talks only to Fresh on your paired iPhone (song names and play state); the DJ Live Activity is shown and updated on the device without any server.

Output test (microphone)

Settings > Output test > Record and compare asks for microphone access only when a USB audio interface is connected, and only when you start that check. Fresh plays a test pattern and records the interface's input in memory to compare it with the pattern; the recording is never saved and never sent. No other part of Fresh uses the microphone.

Spotlight and Siri

Your albums, artists, playlists and your own notes are added to the on-device Spotlight index so you can find them in iOS search; this index stays on the device and is protected like Fresh's other files. Notes appear in search results while the device is unlocked. Deleting a note removes it from search; deleting Fresh removes all of these entries.

Sample songs

“Try sample songs” (shown while your library is empty) copies three short public-domain (CC0) piano recordings that ship inside the app into Fresh's own folder. Nothing is downloaded. “Remove sample songs” deletes them again.

Find by sleeve (camera)

If you choose Find by Sleeve, Fresh asks for camera access. The photo you take is compared on your device with the covers and tags of the albums in your library; it is never saved, added to your photo library or sent anywhere. To make later searches faster, Fresh stores a small numeric summary of each album cover (not of your photo) next to its cover cache.

Lyrics

Fresh reads lyrics embedded in your own music files and matching .lrc or .txt sidecar files on device. If you connect a music server, Fresh also asks that server for the lyrics of songs that came from it. Online lyrics (LRCLIB) are off by default. If you turn them on in Settings and open the lyrics of a song that has none of its own, Fresh sends that song's title, artist, album and length directly to lrclib.net, a community lyrics database, and nothing else: no account, no device identifier and no Fresh server. Found lyrics, and a note when none were found, are cached on your device. Fresh never uploads lyrics to LRCLIB.

Equalizer presets

Fresh does not download headphone measurement data. If you import an equalizer preset file, it is read on your device; a preset you export is saved where you choose. The personal EQ tone test plays tones on your device and keeps only the resulting curve; it is not a hearing test and nothing about it is sent.

Purchases

Fresh uses Apple's StoreKit for the 14-day trial and the one-time purchases Fresh (the player), DJ, and Fresh + DJ. Apple processes the purchase and Apple Account information. Fresh receives signed transaction information, such as the product identifier, original purchase date, verification status, and revocation status, to decide whether playback and the DJ are unlocked. Fresh does not receive your payment-card details. To protect the trial against clock rollback, Fresh stores the latest observed time in the device Keychain. This local record can remain after you delete the app; it is not used to identify or track you.

Feedback

Fresh does not send feedback automatically. If you write feedback and choose Share, Fresh opens an email draft addressed to freshapp.lumi@gmail.com when a mail app is available; otherwise iOS shows a share sheet where you choose the destination. The same address receives optional DJ reports under a separate subject. The shared text contains your message, the Fresh version, and the device operating-system name and version. You can instead copy the same text to the clipboard. You control the final send. Email to this address is handled by Google Gmail; the recipient and email providers receive your sender address and standard email metadata. The privacy practices of the destination you choose apply after sharing. You can contact freshapp.lumi@gmail.com about feedback or privacy requests.

If you enable Help Improve DJ, Fresh stores limited aggregate counters on your device solely to improve DJ playback: sessions, transitions, manual skips, decode failures (also grouped by allowlisted file-format class), recoveries, codec classes, and sample-rate classes. Reports also include the app version, build and operating-system version at export time; counters may span multiple app versions. There are no song titles, artists, file paths, device identifiers, audio recordings or full logs in these counters. The optional DJ transition report is the one exception, and only for the two tracks of the transition it describes: their titles and artists are attached when the "Track names" switch is on. Besides the rating, reasons and comment you give, a transition report contains the transition's type, timing, tempo, key, loudness and vocal measurements for both songs, the planner's scores and alternatives, the recommendation scores, recent DJ playback events (such as rate changes or repairs, with how many seconds ago they happened), your earlier transition ratings with salted song references, your manual DJ picks (for each blend start, entry point, bar nudge, beat-grid or key fix: what the app proposed and what you chose, the tempo and key the app had, and your rating when you gave one, with salted song references; the device keeps the last 2,000 picks and nothing else uses them), the device model, the audio output type (for example headphones or speaker), the audio sample rate and buffer size. Song references are one-time salted hashes that cannot be matched across reports. That switch defaults to on and can be turned off in the report sheet before sending, which removes every track identity from both the message and the attachment. You can stop collection, inspect the report or clear stored counters below Send feedback in Settings. Turning collection off stops new counters; Clear DJ quality data deletes the saved counters. Fresh shares the report only when you choose Share report and a destination; the final send remains under your control. The report uses a separate DJ subject and a versioned JSON format to distinguish it from written feedback. If you send it by email, the recipient and email provider receive your sender address and standard email metadata. Fresh cannot guarantee delivery.

DJ quality report v2 additionally counts personal/manual sessions, recommendation categories, preferences, listening/skip context, transition types/fallback categories and explicit selection/transition quality votes. Votes within 30 seconds of a transition are grouped with its type/fallback, without song identity. This expanded collection requires enabling it again; old counters are preserved. Raw listening history is never included in the report.

For emailed feedback and DJ reports, the developer, a sole proprietor in Turkey (freshapp.lumi@gmail.com), is the data controller. We receive your sender address and name from your mail account and the report content described above. We use them only to answer you and improve DJ features, based on consent or legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)/(f); KVKK Art. 5), never for advertising or sale. We delete emails and reports, including sender details, within 3 months after we reply or the report is processed, whichever comes first; only anonymous aggregate numbers are kept. Write to that address for access, correction or deletion; we answer within one month (GDPR Art. 12) / 30 days (KVKK Art. 13). Your and our mail providers (Google Mail on our side) process email. You may complain to a data-protection authority or the Turkish Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu.

Retention, controls, and deletion

Previously cached covers are preserved. Choosing another folder replaces the saved library reference and library index, but other hashed caches may remain. Deleting Fresh removes its app-container indexes, cached artwork and lyrics, waveform and DJ-analysis caches, settings, and folder reference. Files stored outside Fresh's app container are not deleted by uninstalling Fresh. Apple retains purchase records under Apple's terms so purchases can be restored.

Sharing and sale

Fresh does not sell personal data. It shares only the request data described above with Apple, the music and media servers you connect, ListenBrainz when you connect it, LRCLIB when you turn on online lyrics, and any feedback or mixtape destination you explicitly choose. Fresh does not use this data for cross-app tracking or advertising.

Security and changes

Internet requests use HTTPS; explicitly started Wi-Fi transfers use unencrypted local HTTP. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. This notice may be updated when Fresh's behavior or providers change; the “Last updated” date will change with it.
