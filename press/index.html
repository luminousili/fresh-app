Short description

Fresh is a lossless music player for iPhone and iPad that plays the files you own and the music servers you run, with an optional DJ mode that mixes your library after analysing it on the device.

Description

Fresh plays FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, DSD, Monkey's Audio, WavPack, Opus, Ogg Vorbis, MP3 and AAC from Files, iCloud Drive, USB drives, network folders and your own Jellyfin, Emby, Navidrome / Subsonic or Plex server. Playback is gapless, with ReplayGain and a 16-band equalizer with a parametric editor. Pure playback bypasses every effect, and the Audio path screen shows what still touches the signal; it says “Bit-perfect conditions met” only when every check passes (iOS system sounds can still mix in, and a DAC's volume handling can't be verified).

DJ mode, a separate purchase, mixes from one song into the next. Tempo, beats and key are analysed on the device, and no audio leaves it. Fresh has no account, no ads and no tracking. It runs in CarPlay, on Apple Watch, in widgets and a Live Activity, and in 36 languages.

Highlights

  • Lossless and DSD files, gapless, ReplayGain, 16-band parametric EQ
  • Audio path screen with honest bit-perfect checks
  • DJ mode with on-device tempo, beat and key analysis
  • Your own servers and network folders, offline downloads
  • No account, no tracking; one-time purchases
