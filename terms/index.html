Purchases and trial

Fresh, DJ and Fresh + DJ are one-time in-app purchases sold and processed by Apple. The 14-day trial is a separate purchase at no charge; it never charges or renews automatically. When it ends, playback and the DJ lock until you buy; your library, playlists, stats and exports stay available. The DJ needs Fresh (the player). Refunds are handled by Apple.

Your music

Fresh has no music catalogue. It plays files and servers you choose. You are responsible for having the right to use the music you play, edit, convert or share with Fresh.

Third-party services

Optional features talk to services run by others, under their own terms: LRCLIB for online lyrics, ListenBrainz for scrobbling, plex.tv for Plex sign-in, and the music servers you connect. Jellyfin, Emby, Navidrome, Subsonic and Plex are named only to say which servers Fresh connects to; Fresh is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by those projects.

Analysis results

Tempo, beat, key and other DJ analysis is automatic and can be wrong. “Bit-perfect conditions met” on the Audio path screen means every check Fresh can run has passed; iOS system sounds can still mix in, and your DAC's volume handling can't be verified.
