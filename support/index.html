Fresh Support

Questions, problems or privacy requests: email freshapp.lumi@gmail.com. In the app you can also use Settings > Send feedback.

How do I add my music?

Open Add music (shown when your library is empty, or Settings > Library > Add music) and choose “A folder in Files”: on this iPhone, a USB drive or any cloud drive in the Files app. Fresh plays the music files you own. It never uploads, moves or changes them.

No music yet? “Try sample songs” adds three short public-domain (CC0) piano recordings that ship inside the app. “Remove sample songs” deletes them again.

Can I use my own music server?

Yes. Go to Settings > Music Servers and add your Subsonic-compatible server with its address, user name and password. Fresh contacts only that server. Plain http is allowed only for addresses on your local network. Disconnect in Settings to delete the stored address, user name and password.

How do the trial and purchases work?

Tap “Start 14-day Trial” in Settings > Access Fresh. Try everything, the DJ included, for 14 days. Nothing is charged automatically. When the trial ends, playback and the DJ lock until you buy. Your library, playlists, stats and exports stay available.

You can then buy Fresh (the player), the DJ, or Fresh + DJ. One-time purchases. No subscription.

I bought Fresh before. How do I get it back?

Tap “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen or in Settings > DJ, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I send feedback about the DJ?

Use Settings > Send feedback. Fresh opens an email draft to freshapp.lumi@gmail.com; you control the final send. If you turn on “Help improve DJ”, you can also send a DJ quality report with “Share report”; it goes to the same address under a separate subject. “Clear DJ quality data” deletes the saved counters.

How do I delete my data?

Fresh has no account and no server, so your data stays on your device. Deleting Fresh removes its app-container indexes, cached artwork and lyrics, waveform and DJ-analysis caches, settings, and folder reference. Files stored outside Fresh's app container are not deleted by uninstalling Fresh.

Inside the app: Disconnect a music server in Settings, log out of ListenBrainz in Settings, use “Clear DJ quality data”, or “Remove sample songs”. To protect the trial against clock rollback, Fresh stores the latest observed time in the device Keychain; this local record can remain after you delete the app. See the Privacy Policy for details.
