Frequently asked questions

How do I add my music?

Open Add music (shown while your library is empty, or Settings > Library > Add music) and choose a folder in Files: on this iPhone, a USB drive or a cloud drive such as iCloud Drive. Fresh plays the files where they are; it does not upload or move them. No music yet? “Try sample songs” adds three short public-domain (CC0) piano recordings that ship inside the app; “Remove sample songs” deletes them again.

Which formats does Fresh play?

FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, DSD (DSF and DFF), Monkey's Audio (APE), WavPack, Opus, Ogg Vorbis, MP3 and AAC. DSD plays as PCM by default.

Can I use my own music server?

Yes. Go to Settings > Music Servers and add a Navidrome or Subsonic server (address, user name, password), sign in to Jellyfin or Emby, or sign in with Plex in your browser. Network folders (WebDAV, SMB) and DLNA / UPnP servers are added by address. Plain http is allowed only for addresses on your local network. Disconnect in Settings to delete the stored sign-in.

How do I start the DJ?

Turn on Settings > DJ > DJ mode, then open the DJ tab and tap Start. The first time, Fresh analyses the songs on your device, which takes a few minutes. The DJ is a separate purchase and needs Fresh (the player).

Is playback bit-perfect?

Open the Audio path screen. It says “Bit-perfect conditions met” only when every check it runs passes. Even then, iOS system sounds can still mix in, and your DAC's volume handling can't be verified. To get there: use a USB DAC (Apple's wired adapter works for files up to 48 kHz / 24-bit), turn on Pure playback and keep the DJ off, set the volume to 100% and adjust loudness on your DAC or amplifier, avoid Bluetooth and AirPlay, and close other apps that play audio.

Where do lyrics come from?

From your files (embedded lyrics, or .lrc and .txt files next to them) and from your music server. Online lyrics from LRCLIB are off by default; turn them on in Settings > Playback > Lyrics. Then Fresh sends only the song's title, artist, album and length to lrclib.net.

How do the trial and purchases work?

Tap “Start 14-day Trial” in Settings > Access Fresh to try everything, the DJ included, for 14 days. Nothing is charged automatically. When the trial ends, playback and the DJ lock until you buy; your library, playlists, stats and exports stay available. Then buy Fresh (the player), the DJ, or Fresh + DJ: one-time purchases, no subscription. The paid purchases work with Family Sharing.

I bought Fresh before. How do I get it back?

Tap “Restore Purchases” on the purchase screen or in Settings > DJ, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Fresh has no account and no server, so your data stays on your device. Deleting Fresh removes its indexes, caches, settings and folder reference; your own music files outside Fresh are not deleted. To protect the trial against clock rollback, Fresh keeps the latest observed time in the device Keychain, and this record can remain after you delete the app. Details are in the privacy policy.

Jellyfin, Emby, Navidrome, Subsonic and Plex are named only to say which servers Fresh connects to. Fresh is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by those projects.
